AgendaPeek Support

AgendaPeek brings calendar events and dated reminders to your iPhone Home Screen, one week at a time.

Contact

For support, privacy questions, or to report an inappropriate advertisement, email xuanjinw789@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. If you attach screenshots, remove private calendar and reminder information first.

Add a Home Screen widget

Open AgendaPeek and grant the permissions you want to use. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, choose Edit and Add Widget (or the plus button on older iOS versions), search for AgendaPeek, and add a widget. Touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to select Days 1–7, Days 8–14, or Days 15–21.

Missing items or a widget that has not updated

Open AgendaPeek, check Access at the bottom of settings, and confirm the intended calendars and reminder lists are enabled. Reminders need a date or time and must be incomplete. Check the selected week and the past-calendar-event display setting. iOS controls widget refresh timing; changes may not appear immediately. If necessary, remove and re-add the widget.

Language and layout

Use the globe-marked Language setting to follow your system language or choose one of the supported languages. Column count, font sizes, and title lines apply to all widgets. Each widget chooses its own week. Small widgets display one day.

Advertising and privacy

The app includes a banner ad; widgets do not contain ads. Use the controls on an ad to view information or report it, or contact us. Where applicable, revisit ad privacy choices from Privacy at the bottom of app settings. Calendar and reminder content is not added to advertising requests. Read the AgendaPeek Privacy Policy for details.

Manage permissions

Use iPhone Settings to change AgendaPeek’s Calendar and Reminders permissions. Removing the app does not remove your original events or reminders from their source apps.
