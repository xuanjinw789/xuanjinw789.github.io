AgendaPeek Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Developer and privacy contact: Xuanjin Wang
Email: xuanjinw789@gmail.com

This policy describes how AgendaPeek, its iPhone app, and its Home Screen widgets handle information. “We” and “our” refer to the developer identified above.

Calendar events and reminders

With your permission, AgendaPeek reads information available through Apple’s Calendar and Reminders services on your device to display your agenda. This includes event and reminder titles, dates and times, event end dates, all-day status, reminder completion status, calendar/list names, calendar account labels, and identifiers used to filter or open items. AgendaPeek displays incomplete reminders that have a date or time.

The app processes this information on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your calendar or reminder content. The app does not add that content to advertising requests, sell it, or use it to target ads. AgendaPeek does not edit or delete your calendar events or reminders.

You can exclude individual calendars and reminder lists in the app. These filters control what AgendaPeek displays and processes for its agenda; they do not narrow the underlying access permission granted by iOS. You can revoke Calendar or Reminders access in iPhone Settings at any time. Features that rely on that permission will then be limited.

Calendars and reminders synced by Apple or another account provider remain subject to that provider’s settings and policies. AgendaPeek does not ask for your Google, Microsoft, or Apple account password or independently sign you in to those services.

Preferences and widgets

AgendaPeek saves preferences on your device, including language, layout, font sizes, past-event display settings, and excluded calendar/list identifiers. The app and widget extension share these preferences using Apple’s App Group storage. Widgets use permitted calendar and reminder information to render their displays. iOS may cache rendered widget content and controls refresh timing.

AgendaPeek does not require an app account. We have not added a separate developer-operated analytics or cloud-sync service. Advertising services described below may still process usage and diagnostic data.

Advertising and Google services

AgendaPeek uses Google AdMob to display banner ads in the app. Its widgets do not include an advertising SDK. Google’s advertising SDK may collect or receive IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or app identifiers, diagnostics, performance information, advertising activity, and interactions with the app or ads. Google may use this data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

The current app requests non-personalized ads. This does not mean advertising services collect no data or that consent is never required. AgendaPeek does not request Apple’s tracking permission or directly read the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google’s User Messaging Platform checks which privacy messages and choices apply. Where required, it presents a consent form and makes Ad privacy choices available under Privacy in the app. You can use that control to revisit the choices offered for your region. Some privacy choices may affect ad availability.

Google’s handling of information, service providers, and available controls is described in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Google is required to handle information under applicable privacy obligations and its service terms. This policy does not grant permission to use your calendar or reminder content for advertising.

Privacy and support website

Our privacy and support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting infrastructure may process request information such as your IP address and browser information to deliver and secure the pages. We do not add advertising or third-party analytics scripts to these pages. Hosting is subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement. Please email our privacy contact if you have questions about information we control.

Opening items and external links

When you tap an agenda item, AgendaPeek may ask iOS to open the relevant Calendar or Reminders app using an item identifier or date. Those apps operate under their own policies. Tapping an ad or another external link may open a third-party website or app. That service may receive information about your visit; its privacy policy applies to that interaction.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue, and handle privacy requests. Please avoid sending sensitive calendar content unless necessary for your request. Email is handled by our email provider as well as by us.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your underlying events and reminders remain in their original Calendar or Reminders stores. Manage or delete them in those apps. AgendaPeek reads them to render the agenda rather than creating a developer-hosted archive. Content already rendered by iOS in a widget may remain visible until the widget refreshes or is removed.

App preferences remain in local/shared app storage until changed or removed by iOS. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, generally removes its app data; system backups, shared storage, and cached widget displays may be managed separately by iOS. Removing AgendaPeek does not delete your original calendar events or reminders. Remove its widgets if you no longer want agenda content displayed on your Home Screen.

Google manages retention and deletion of advertising data under its policies and the privacy choices applicable to you; uninstalling AgendaPeek does not itself delete information Google has already received. Use the Google privacy resources linked above and the app’s available ad privacy choices to manage that information.

We retain support correspondence for up to 12 months after the issue is resolved, then delete it from our active mailbox. We may retain relevant messages longer when necessary to comply with legal obligations or resolve an ongoing dispute, and delete them when that need ends. Copies retained by our email provider in backups or recovery systems are subject to that provider’s retention practices. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you. We will respond as required by applicable law. We cannot delete items in your calendar account or information independently controlled by Google; we can explain where to direct those requests.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or raise a concern with a privacy regulator. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of prior processing. Contact the privacy email above for assistance.

Processing locations and safeguards

Calendar and reminder content used by AgendaPeek is processed on your device. Advertising and support-email providers may process their respective information in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ. Their policies describe their processing locations and safeguards.

AgendaPeek relies on iOS permissions and app storage protections and does not deliberately include agenda content in advertising requests. Keep your iPhone protected and consider what information is visible on your Home Screen. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children’s privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request or has a privacy concern involving this app, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Parents or guardians can manage app permissions and device access through iOS settings.

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version. We will provide additional notice or request consent where required.
